# UniLend — Site Vision

## 1. Product

UniLend is a Vietnamese university community platform where students discover, borrow, and share useful items with people nearby on campus. It helps people access what they need while making better use of items that would otherwise sit unused.

## 2. Audience

University students looking for affordable, convenient access to study, technology, and everyday items, as well as students who want to share items with their campus community.

## 3. Voice and visual direction

Write in natural Vietnamese with correct diacritics. Be friendly, direct, and community-minded. The visual direction is **Sky Athletic** (Nike-inspired): sky blue on bright, clean whites, huge uppercase condensed headlines, full-bleed photography, pill buttons, and a minimal, energetic storefront. (Replaces the earlier green "Modern Commerce" direction.)
