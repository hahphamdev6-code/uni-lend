Create a polished desktop **Explore** page for **UniLend**, the Vietnamese campus community platform where students discover, borrow, and share useful items nearby. It must match the Home screen style: Nike-inspired Sky Athletic — bold, minimal, energetic, and image-led. Use correct Vietnamese text with diacritics and high-quality natural product photography.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Responsive web, desktop-first with a carefully adapted mobile layout.
- Visual direction: Sky Athletic — inspired by Nike.com: bold, minimal, energetic, image-led, huge uppercase headlines, full-bleed photography, generous white space.
- Background: Pure White (#FFFFFF); alternate sections and image wells in Cloud White (#F5F8FB).
- Brand: Sky Blue (#2BA8F0) for large color blocks and highlights; Ocean Blue (#0568B5) for primary pill buttons, links, active states; Ice Blue (#E3F3FD) for soft badges and chips.
- Text: Ink (#111111) headlines and nav; Steel Gray (#707072) secondary text. Hairline borders (#E5E5E5).
- Typography: Extra-bold condensed UPPERCASE display sans (Nike Futura–style, e.g., Barlow Condensed/Oswald) with tight line-height; clean neutral grotesk for UI (Helvetica Now–style, e.g., Inter/Be Vietnam Pro). Vietnamese diacritics must render correctly.
- Components: Fully pill-shaped buttons and chips, product cards with square images on Cloud White backdrops and text below, minimal radius on images (0–8 px), hairline dividers, no shadows.
- Imagery: Large, crisp, natural photography of useful student-owned items and students on a bright campus; images dominate.
- Avoid: gradients, glassmorphism, heavy shadows, green or warm accent colors, generic dashboard styling, prices or sales language.

**PAGE STRUCTURE:**
1. **Promo strip:** Thin Cloud White bar: “Mượn miễn phí trong khuôn viên — Hẹn gặp trong 5 phút”.
2. **Header:** Identical to Home — UNILEND wordmark, nav links with “Khám phá” active, search pill, “Đăng nhập”, and black pill “Tham gia”.
3. **Hero / Search bar:** Condensed UPPERCASE title “KHÁM PHÁ ĐỒ DÙNG QUANH BẠN”, one short subtitle, and a prominent search bar (“Bạn đang cần tìm món gì?”) with distance selector (“Gần bạn · Bán kính 1km”) and Ocean Blue pill “Tìm kiếm”.
4. **Filter & Sort bar:** Pill chips “Tất cả” (active), “Học tập”, “Công nghệ”, “Đời sống”, “Sáng tạo & Nhiếp ảnh”; compact dropdowns “Tình trạng: Có sẵn”, “Bán kính”, “Sắp xếp: Gần nhất”; item counter “48 món đồ trong khuôn viên”.
5. **Left filter sidebar (desktop):** Clean checkboxes for availability, distance, meeting spot types (Thư viện, KTX, Căn-tin), and verified owners. Collapsible into a pill “Bộ lọc” button on mobile.
6. **Product catalog (3-column grid):** 9–12 cards matching the Home styling (square photo on Cloud White backdrop, Sky Blue “Có sẵn hôm nay” badge, bold item name, category & distance in Steel Gray, student owner line, and pill “Chi tiết & Đặt mượn”). Include 1 card with status “Đang được mượn” (return date indicator).
7. **Pagination / Infinite scroll trigger:** Centered outline pill button “Xem thêm món đồ”.
8. **Footer:** Identical to Home.

**QUALITY BAR:** Never name a real university, real brand café, or price. No checkout or sales language. Keep the look crisp, white-dominant, and athletic.
